- Location and dates: Austin, Texas, May 2026 – August 2026 (completed).

- Overlay correction tool: engineered a Python-based tool that converted 500+ wafer- and shot-level overlay measurements per lot into scanner corrections, resolving multi-layer misalignments and saving about 250 engineering hours a year.

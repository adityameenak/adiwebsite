# Projects

## STEM Research Finder

- Role: co-creator and lead developer. Live at stemresearchfinder.tech.
- What it is: a search engine for undergraduate research. It pulls faculty profiles from university websites across the country into one place, so students can search by school, field or research topic instead of trawling department pages.
- Scale: indexes 5,000+ STEM faculty nationwide; 3,000+ student users, grown without paid marketing.
- How it works: Python data pipelines across 50+ university web sources extract and standardize faculty research profiles, deduplicate records, and automate index updates. It is multi-tenant, supporting university-specific deployments.
- Features: resume-based faculty matching and personalized outreach-email drafting, connecting a student's experience with relevant research labs.
- Tech: Python, data pipelines / web scraping, Next.js, React.
